How to read the numbers

  • Measured Real-world usage data or a scientific measurement.
  • Poll A published poll. Mostly US or UK adults, and wording varies, so treat it as a snapshot. When a poll reports both sides, the bar shows the split among people who picked one.
  • Educated guess No reliable data found. It’s an estimate, so vote and argue with it.

The live people counter

Each card multiplies its percentage by an estimated live world population (about 8.3 billion in mid-2026, growing roughly 2 people per second). It shows what the planet would look like if everyone answered like the poll. It is an extrapolation, not a headcount.
